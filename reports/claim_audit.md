# Paper claim audit

| Existing claim | Evidence | Audit result | Action | Safer wording |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Gate rejects 93,154 railed samples | `data/phytosense_trial_log.csv`, `bioelectric_raw >= 4090` | Verified as source-row count; duplicates reduce this to 93,049 unique timestamps. Main CSV lacks per-cycle `n_sat`, so firmware's mixed-saturation rail condition cannot be reconstructed. | Qualify | “The source CSV contains 93,154 rail-valued rows; after duplicate timestamp coalescing, 93,049 unique rail timestamps remain.” |
| Rail samples form one Oct 13–14 episode | Timestamp grouping in revised episode CSV | Not supported: rail-valued rows exist in September and multiple October groups. At a 10-second inter-row merge rule, 109 operational episodes are found. | Remove | “Rail-valued observations occur in multiple episodes; episode boundaries depend on the stated 10-second merge rule.” |
| Gate prevents known rails entering baseline | Firmware gate plus logged means | Offline replay admitted 93,049/93,049 unique rail points ungated and 0 gated under available-value approximation. This assumes baseline initialization at file start and infers rail from logged average; it is not deployed firmware telemetry. | Qualify | “In an offline firmware-like replay initialized at the beginning of the CSV, the available-value gate rejects all logged rail averages.” |
| The baseline recovers after real faults | CSV sessions and gaps | Continuous recovery cannot be measured reliably because timestamps have long gaps, no timezone, missing raw ADC oversamples, and unknown device state across sessions. | Remove | “This dataset does not establish real-time recovery after the rail episode.” |
| Five quality checks are implemented | `firmware/src/main.cpp` | Confirmed: contact, high rail, low rail, 60-sample flatline, and jump. Existing full logs do not expose every input (notably `n_sat`). | Retain with qualification | “Firmware implements five gate checks; historical field availability limits retrospective reconstruction.” |
| Existing benchmark validates detector ranking and event latency | Legacy `analysis/benchmark.py`, `out/bench_*.csv` | Outputs are row-based, duplicates are retained, gaps are dropped then row time is treated as seconds, event hits include the guard interval, and reported latency is an index. Outputs predate this audit and provenance is absent. | Remove pending rerun | “Legacy benchmark outputs are exploratory synthetic-event results and are not suitable for elapsed-time performance claims.” |
| Synthetic shifts demonstrate plant-stress detection | Existing and revised synthetic injections | No biological ground truth or labelled plant-stress events are present. | Remove | “Synthetic shifts test software behavior only; plant-stress detection is unvalidated.” |
| Fixed-window mean absorption equation | Firmware rolling arithmetic mean | Derivation is valid under fixed full buffer and fixed scale assumptions. Actual firmware scale adapts and detector persistence/gating affect alarm clearing. | Retain with assumptions | “For a fixed-scale rolling-mean reference model, residual shift is |δ|(1−k/N); this is not a closed-form alarm-clearing law for the full firmware.” |
| ESP32-S3 computation cost comparisons | Legacy `bench_cost.csv` | The table is theoretical state/op estimates, not measured execution time. Operation descriptions are not directly comparable across algorithms. | Qualify | “Estimated buffer/state memory only; no board timing comparison is established by these outputs.” |

Do not silently change paper quantitative tables until the revised benchmark is fully rerun under a documented elapsed-time protocol and independently reviewed.
